# 1.2 / Using Tech Wiki

Tech Wiki operates similarly to Wikipedia, allowing technical team members to contribute to sections that align with their strengths while also exploring and developing knowledge in areas where they seek growth. It serves as a central platform for sharing information and continuously strengthening our department’s collective expertise.

There is no login required to access Tech Wiki. Simply open it via the bookmark bar on any Tech Department PC or MacBook. Team members are encouraged to freely contribute and edit content, with all updates subject to review by the Manager and Supervisors.

As you navigate the wiki, feel free to:

* Add questions in comment bubble where information may need further clarification
* Contribute additional details or insights
* Upload relevant images or supporting materials

Your input helps ensure the wiki remains accurate, useful, and up to date for everyone.
