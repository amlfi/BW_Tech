# 6.9.3.2 / During Show

**Your Role as a Technical Operator**

As the Technical Operator, it is your responsibility to actively monitor the camera system during the following hours:

* Tuesday to Thursday: **9:00 AM – 4:00 PM**
* Friday to Sunday: **9:00 AM – 6:00 PM**

This task is a critical function that directly supports the safety and well-being of our Experience Team, Front of House team, Merch Team and F\&B Team. Your attention helps ensure that our staff, who are working face-to-face with the public, feel supported and protected throughout their day.

You must remain attentive at all times. Supervisors from other departments may call on you for urgent matters — **including, but not limited to**:

* Managing rowdy or disruptive groups
* Monitoring large or crowded groups
* Addressing patrons taking photos or videos in restricted areas
* Reviewing incidents that may have occurred on-site

When you’re watching the cameras, you’re not just observing — you’re providing peace of mind to your co-workers, acting as an extra set of eyes and a crucial point of contact in moments of uncertainty.

This role carries real responsibility. Please approach it with focus, empathy, and the understanding that your vigilance helps protect your team and the quality of our venue experience.
