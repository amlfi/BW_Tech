# 6.6 / Win Script

## How to launch.

The Winscript live program resides on the Show Control Server and is accessible through the dock.

Open WinScript Live and await the display of the splash screen. Click on "Open"—currently, the most recently saved ShowFile—then select "Connect." When prompted for a password, enter "Bluey." Next, input the address for establishing a connection to Showtouch and network configuration.



## How to Save

| **Save As** | Go to **File > Save As**. Choose a location, enter a filename, and save |
| ----------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |

## How to Connect Live

|  **Configure Connection** | **Open Connection Manager** | Click the **"Connect"** button in the software's menu bar.                                    | Scans the network for devices.                 |
| ------------------------- | --------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
|                           | **Pair the Device**         | In the list, select your V16X. Click **"Change Name"** to match the name in your open script. | Pairs the physical hardware to your project.   |
| **Connect Hardware**      | **Send Project**            | Click **"Send Project"** to upload the script to the V16X. The device will restart.           | Your show logic is loaded onto the controller. |
|                           | **Enter Live Mode**         | Once online with green checkmarks, click **"Connect in Live Mode"**.                          | Establish real-time control and monitoring.    |
