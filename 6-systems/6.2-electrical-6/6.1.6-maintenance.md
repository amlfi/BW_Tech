<summary>Cleaning The Projectors</summary>

Tools Required:

* Phillips Head Screwdriver
* Microfibre Cloth
* Makita Air Gun\
  <br>

Safety Precautions:

* Ensure the projector is turned off and unplugged.
* Allow the projector to cool down completely before starting.
* Work in a clean, static-free environment to prevent damage to internal components.\
  <br>

Step-by-Step Cleaning Guide:

Remove the Right-Hand Side Panel:

* Using the Phillips head screwdriver, remove the six black screws from the right-hand side panel (when facing the projector front-on).
* Carefully detach the side panel and set it aside.<br>

Access the Left-Hand Side:

* On the left-hand side, remove the bottom three screws, leaving the top three screws in place.<br>

Remove the Bottom Plate:

* Remove all screws from the bottom panel.
* Gently remove the bottom plate to expose the internal components.<br>

Detach the Front Panel:

* With the underside visible, locate and remove the screws securing the front panel.
* Carefully remove the front panel, noting that there is a clip on the top that may require gentle pressure to release.<br>

Clean the Panels:

* Take the removed front and side panels outside or to a well-ventilated area.
* Use the air gun to blow off any dust and debris from the panels.
* Wipe the panels with a microfibre cloth to remove any remaining dust particles.



Reassemble the Projector:

* Reattach the front panel, ensuring the top clip is securely fastened.
* Replace the bottom plate and secure it with the screws.
* Reinsert the three screws on the left-hand side.
* Reattach the right-hand side panel with the six screws.<br>

Additional Tips:

* Avoid using chemical cleaners or solvents, as they may damage the projector's surfaces.
* Do not attempt to clean the internal optical components unless you are trained to do so, as improper handling can cause damage.
* Regularly check and clean the projector's air filters, if applicable, to maintain proper airflow and cooling.

<summary>Chattermax Config </summary>

Physical Setup.

Ensure that the distance from the floor to the face of each corner speaker is approximately 3.1m (tolerance of up to 30mm.

Projector Setup

Open the menu and navigate to the test pattern menu.&#x20;

<img src="../../.gitbook/assets/unknown.png" alt="" data-size="original">![](<../../.gitbook/assets/unknown (1).png>)

-Scroll through and select the Focus Pattern

-Once the Focus Pattern is displaying on the wall go back and make your way to the Position Menu and select Corner Correction

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/unknown (2).png>)![](<../../.gitbook/assets/unknown (3).png>)

Once in the Corner Correction menu select one corner at a time and align it with the wall ensuring that the “T” is flush with the wall.



1. It is important to note that on the bottom of the wall the top of the “T” wants to be flush with floor and on the Top of the wall the bottom of the “T” wants to be flush with the wall
2. It is also important to note that each wall will have a significant overlap in the centre where both projectors hit the same spot. This overlap is larger on the short walls than it is on the long walls.

Computer Setup&#x20;

1. Assuming the computer has just been freshly booted you want to locate and open Blackmagic Desktop Video Setup from the desktop

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/unknown (4).png>)![](<../../.gitbook/assets/unknown (5).png>)

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/unknown (6).png>)



When the software opens you should see the screen pictured on the bottom left. Navigate to the bottom right hand corner and click on the 3 lines. This will bring you to the list view where you can confirm that all the video outputs are correctly formatted. (It should look as picture on the right)



Software Setup

1. Launch Madmapper and open the most recent show file
2. Currently “Blueys World Chattermax Showfile 02.11.2025” ![](<../../.gitbook/assets/unknown (8).png>)![](<../../.gitbook/assets/unknown (9).png>)



1. Once the showfile has loaded click on the full screen icon as highlighted in the red circle
2. When the icon is yellow it means full screen is enabled
3. Now that the show is in Fullscreen mode the selected scene should be displayed onto the walls in Chattermax. The final step is to make sure we have the correct scene selected
4. At the bottom of the screenshot above you will see 4 boxes, 2 Black and 2 Pink ones.
5. The Black ones are for Version 1
6. The Pink ones are for Version 2
7. If you hover the mouse over the far right pink box you will see a little play button. Simply click this play button to activate this scene and the show controller will do the rest
