# 6.9.3.1 / Start Up

<details>

<summary>🔌 Open Procedure – Macarthur Entry Nook</summary>

**Turn on Power**

* Go to the Macarthur Entry Nook Distro.
* Switch **Breaker 5** to the ON position.

**Check the TV and Raspberry Pi**

* The **TV and Raspberry Pi** should turn on automatically.
* Wait about a minute for the Raspberry Pi to fully start up.

**Check NX Witness**

* NX Witness should launch automatically and log in.
* If NX Doesn't Auto Launch Click on the Raspberry in the Top Left Corner of the desktop and select NX Witness from the "Other" Tab
* If it **doesn’t log in**, click on **“Blueys World” serve**r.\
  Use the **Fairy\_Mac login details** from **Airtable**.

**Open the Correct Layout**

* At the top of the NX Witness screen, click the **dropdown next to the “+” icon**.\
  Choose **“Fairy Cupboard (Mac)”**.
* Once that layout is showing, you're **SHOW READY.**



</details>

<details>

<summary>🔌 Open Procedure – River Entry Nook</summary>

**Turn on Power**

* Go to the River Entry Nook Distro.
* Switch **Breaker 1** to the ON position.

**Check the TV and Raspberry Pi**

* The **TV and Raspberry Pi** should turn on automatically.
* Wait a moment for the Raspberry Pi to start.

**Check NX Witness**

* NX Witness should open and log in automatically.
* If NX Doesn't Auto Launch Click on the Raspberry in the Top Left Corner of the desktop and select NX Witness from the "Other" Tab
* If it **doesn’t log in**, click on **“Blueys World” server**.
* Use the **Fairy\_Riv login detail**s from **Airtable**.

**Open the Correct Layout**

* Click the **dropdown menu next to the “+” icon**.\
  Select **“Fairy Cupboard (Riv)”**.
* Once the layout appears, you're **SHOW READY**.

</details>

<details>

<summary>🔌 Open Procedure – Experience Office</summary>

**Turn on the TV**

* The Raspberry Pi and feed should already be running.
* **Just turn the TV on**.

**If the Raspberry Pi is Off**

* Press the **power button** on the Raspberry Pi.

**Check NX Witness**

* NX Witness should open and log in automatically.
* If NX Doesn't Auto Launch Click on the Raspberry in the Top Left Corner of the desktop and select NX Witness from the "Other" Tab
* If it **doesn’t**, click **“Blueys World” server**.
* Use the **Experience Office login details** from **Airtable**.

**Open the Correct Layout**

* Use the **dropdown next to the “+” icon**.
* Select **“Experience Layout”**.
* Once the layout loads, you're **SHOW READY**.

</details>
