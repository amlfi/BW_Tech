## How to Configure Luminex Nodes in UniFi



Learn how to access and configure network ports ready for use with Luminex Nodes.

1\. Navigate to [https://unifi.ui.com/network-servers/b90629c5-e1bf-4148-9e72-c213ad63b5a3/network/c9j8ilay](https://unifi.ui.com/network-servers/b90629c5-e1bf-4148-9e72-c213ad63b5a3/network/c9j8ilay)

![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-03-27/5885bc4f-d9a0-4f6d-83f7-8448b3cb6678/ascreenshot_04179d003c204805a6c6f3821099b91d_text_export.jpeg)

2\. Click "[portalfi-mgmt.tbdproductions.com.au](http://portalfi-mgmt.tbdproductions.com.au)"

![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-03-27/5885bc4f-d9a0-4f6d-83f7-8448b3cb6678/ascreenshot_0fc70352852245b2a99ad622d67cd192_text_export.jpeg)

3\. Click this icon.

![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-03-27/54791f73-e8fa-432f-928f-1496f4cf5abc/ascreenshot_195795dbe8384518a1035209885e6b9c_text_export.jpeg)

4\. Click the "Ports" Icon

![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-03-27/57b8b244-b0df-435b-9bc4-10283e0bc50b/ascreenshot_2104a0b031904e54b6a0d07d1d7fcc9e_text_export.jpeg)

5\. Open the drop down menu

![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-03-27/73e7793b-9d93-4aa4-a1e7-3ae7412ca87c/ascreenshot_786fabe5b3e942fc9aabacf70aaf59e4_text_export.jpeg)

6\. Click on the switch with the connected device

![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-03-27/c50e876b-51a1-40c3-8e84-09fa5f2c7086/ascreenshot_1dab8f00504546a5a5aa0b079c181f37_text_export.jpeg)

7\. Identify the device

![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-03-27/40d8af20-0262-4aaa-bb84-3ac8d3aa1437/user_cropped_screenshot_6486b47f6ba840ca95bcab41afb72a87_text_export.jpeg)

8\. Double check that Device information is correct

![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-03-27/f8ddb725-6784-4cd9-91c4-d3f825613592/user_cropped_screenshot_a1535c4c93db411db678618675e5065a_text_export.jpeg)

9\. Close the device tab

![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-03-27/09d55a2a-2e4e-4065-8e55-31fb7f1e3ce0/ascreenshot_d5e7a6fc6a6f41fb84fad805954f51da_text_export.jpeg)

10\. Click on the port above or below the device

![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-03-27/b56d5f33-9617-4dc4-b97f-0edce8f485d6/ascreenshot_2ba2a85ad9274654a7c5d0335d549c49_text_export.jpeg)

11\. Ensure the port is active

![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-03-27/838ab568-6f92-4b77-a9e8-9f2e90fb5e4b/ascreenshot_a7f38b4f36f1417fbd83145ff214ca04_text_export.jpeg)

12\. Open the VLan drop down menu

![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-03-27/2d6752f0-c7a6-438a-82f7-2fac6266f082/ascreenshot_ebada16b0bfa4c9591f88e64cfb77b17_text_export.jpeg)

13\. Select VLan 08 - DMX SCN

![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-03-27/9ccc5ebd-64ef-4474-892f-36e206d7fc2c/ascreenshot_0626f75d89424f7abd17d3e3355e8c97_text_export.jpeg)

14\. Ensure Tagged VLAN Management is set to Allow All

![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-03-27/08e094e9-36dc-40df-8877-4553871c1e62/ascreenshot_4d2a599e08b54b1bac891a830e4f0291_text_export.jpeg)

15\. If the device requires PoE now is you to enable it

![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-03-27/30735e1d-4247-4909-b8ab-4526cea58974/ascreenshot_562f17174a8441db95d4b5ff6c64db36_text_export.jpeg)

16\. Advanced settings can be set to "Auto" in this instance

![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-03-27/602cfcb2-905b-4ee0-87c9-d5419cf98b24/ascreenshot_53732f72891940cc8310d09955b4cf78_text_export.jpeg)

17\. Close the Port Settings tab and wait a few moments

![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-03-27/54dee7b8-6b64-4423-a083-42594d9f96cc/ascreenshot_4d52f33c1e354d018d488dc6cb924aba_text_export.jpeg)
