# 3.5 / Technical Startup/Shutdown Checklists

### 🧠 Core Idea of Shutdown

Shutdown isn’t just “turn everything off.”

You are balancing **three priorities**:

1. **Show is finished → remove all show systems**
2. **Cleaners need a safe environment → light + access**
3. **Next day needs a reset → equipment charged, nothing left running**

### ⏱ When Shutdown Happens

* You **start gradually** after patrons leave Meet & Greet
* You **finish fully** only after last patron leaves

👉 Why:

* You don’t disrupt guest experience
* You avoid rushing everything at the end

### 💡 Lighting Decisions

* Show lights → OFF
* Work lights → ON

👉 Why:

* Show lighting is not functional
* Work lights give **clear, safe visibility** for cleaners

### 🚪 Maglocks

* Maglocks → OFF

👉 Why:

* Cleaners need **free movement**
* Locked doors = unsafe + inefficient

### 🎥 Projectors Logic

* Turn OFF:
  * Chattermax
  * Creek
* Keep ON (initially):
  * Dome

👉 Why:

* Dome acts as **ambient lighting**
* If you turn it off too early → space becomes dark

Later:

* Once cleaners are done → Dome can go OFF

### ⚡ Distros (Power)

* GREEN → OFF
* RED → STAY ON



### 🏠 House Shutdown Flow

You move **space by space**:

* Cubby
* Theatre
* Living room
* River areas
* Front



### 🎛 Systems (Tab 2)

* Resolume → OFF
* Go Photos → OFF

👉 Why:

* Shutting down Resolume helps it run smoother the following day and prevents crashing
* Must be fully reset for next day

### 🔊 Final Guest-Dependent Systems

ONLY after last patron leaves:

* Neighbourhood music → OFF
* Ice cream TV → OFF

### 🔌 Equipment Reset

* Radios → OFF + charge
* iPad → charge

### Final Check&#x20;

* Check cameras
* Look for:
  * Lights left on
  * Systems still running

👉 Why:

* This is your **safety net**
* Catches everything you missed



Shutdown =

> Turn off the _experience_\
> Keep the _environment usable_\
> Reset the _operation for tomorrow_
