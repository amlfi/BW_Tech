# 4.1.1 / Typical Show Rundown

### **⏱️** PRE-SHOW

**7:00-7:30AM**: Tech arrives on site

**7:00 - 8:00AM**: Technical Venue Open

**8:30AM**: SM/ASM arrives with gardeners for mic check

**8:30-8:45AM**: Pre-show technical checks complete: Notify on Channel 4 that the show is tech ready.

***

### **⏱️** FIRST SESSION

#### **8:30AM**: During school holidays/peak days

#### **9:00AM**: Regular days



* FOH will radio when doors are open. Channel #2
* Stage Manager will radio when the first show of the day has started. Channel #4

***

### **⏱️** SHOWS START

Radio set to **Channel 4** (Experience + Tech) for calls&#x20;

Monitor security camera feeds for any unwelcome behaviour such as taking photos/videos in prohibited areas, rude interactions with the actors.&#x20;



<details>

<summary>COMMUNICATION</summary>

Each room has an iPad that allows the Experience team to request assistance.

* Call the FOH Corridor (**Channel 2**) corresponding to the side of the house the call came from.&#x20;
* Check camera footage to see who pressed the iPad. If it’s a false alarm (patron pressing the iPad), convey this to FOH as soon as possible. If cleaning is required, FOH will call them after investigating the room.\


**`Person Alert`** - Patrons taking photos/videos, patrons needing the bathroom.

`Room Alert` - Technical issue in the room such as door maglock, lights or audio not working as they should. Cleaning required in the room due to vomit or spill. Set or prop damage.

`Emergency Alert` - Medical emergency, aggressive or violent behaviour, electrical or fire hazard, immediate safety concerns, power outage/failure.
